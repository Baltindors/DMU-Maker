OVERVIEW

Endocrine resistance remains a major challenge in ER+/HER2- metastatic breast cancer, often driven by acquired ESR1 mutations. Early detection using ctDNA-based testing is critical to guide timely use of next-generation oral SERDs

Join this live, expert-led webinar featuring an oncologist and pathologist as they share practical strategies for managing ESR1 mutation-driven resistance in clinical practice.

Through interactive polling, case discussions, and live Q&A, you will:

  • Optimize ctDNA testing and ESR1 mutation monitoring
  • Review emerging evidence for next-generation oral SERDs
  • Learn how to sequence oral SERDs effectively

Engage with experts in real time and access downloadable reference slides to support informed treatment decisions for patients with endocrine-resistant, ESR1-mutated metastatic breast cancer.
