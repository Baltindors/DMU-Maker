OVERVIEW

Timely genetic testing is critical for identifying patients with germline BRCA-mutated breast cancer. However, testing remains underutilized for many eligible patients.

Join this Live Webinar on Nov 10, led by two expert breast cancer oncologists, for a meaningful discussion on genetic counseling and testing in germline BRCA-mutated breast cancer.

In this live session, you will have the opportunity to:

  • Engage with expert faculty and gain practical insights on optimizing genetic testing
  • Apply guideline-aligned practices for identifying eligible patients for genetic counseling and testing
  • Strengthen approaches for multidisciplinary management for patients with germline BRCA-mutated breast cancer.

In addition, this session will introduce the Breast Cancer Genetic Testing Toolkit, a set of downloadable resources designed to support clinicians and patients in navigating genetic counseling and testing, treatment decisions, and shared decision-making.

Don't miss this opportunity to enhance your clinical expertise and gain practical tools to optimize outcomes for your patients with germline BRCA-mutated breast cancer.
