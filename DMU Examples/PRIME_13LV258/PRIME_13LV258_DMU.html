OVERVIEW

In recent years, significant advances in clinical research on eosinophilic esophagitis (EoE) have yielded deeper understanding of its underlying pathogenesis, including the central roles of epithelial cytokines in disease onset and progression. These new insights have led to exciting developments in novel therapeutic strategies that target underlying drivers of inflammation.

Join renowned experts for an engaging live symposium where they will discuss the latest updates in the diagnosis and treatment of EoE.

This interactive 1.5-hour session will feature:

  • A deep dive into the role of epithelial cytokines in EoE through an innovative animation, with interactive pathway points where you can test your knowledge on this topic
  • Diagnostic dilemmas: competitive collaborative breakout sessions exploring real-world clinical scenarios (featuring video vignettes from adolescents and adults with EoE)
  • Interactive polling and live Q&A for direct engagement with faculty.

Don't miss this opportunity to enhance your clinical expertise, engage with leading gastroenterologists, and see how your knowledge and skills stack up against your peers.
