OVERVIEW

With functional cure for hepatitis B (hep B) on the horizon, providers must be prepared to integrate therapeutic advances and changing guidelines into personalized care for people living with hep B.

Join us in person or via livestream for this interactive symposium on the latest updates in Hep B treatment led by an expert panel featuring Dr. Su Wang, Dr. Mark Sulkowski, and Dr. Yasmin Ibrahim, who represent the Hepatitis B Foundation.

Don't miss out on:

  • Expert-guided review of the latest evidence and guidelines
  • Real-world insights from people living with hep B
  • Mock Clinical Consultations demonstrating effective patient-provider communication strategies
  • A downloadable Shared Decision-Making Guide to use with people living with hep B in your practice
  • Opportunities to engage with the experts through live Q&A.
